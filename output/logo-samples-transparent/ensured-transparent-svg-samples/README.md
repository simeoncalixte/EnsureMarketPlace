# Ensured — transparent SVG logo samples

Four concepts, each supplied as a horizontal logo and standalone icon, in forest/charcoal and mint/cream versions (16 SVG files). All backgrounds are transparent. Lettering is outlined; no external fonts or raster images are required. Light versions are intended for dark backgrounds. Open index.html to compare.

- **Shelter E:** An open shield with three deliberate bars for coverage and clarity.
- **Exchange:** Two opposing brackets connect risk and capital.
- **Foundation:** A structural E with a rising silhouette and a dependable base.
- **Confluence:** Two flowing forms meet in one compact mark.
