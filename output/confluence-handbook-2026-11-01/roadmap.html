Practical sequence

  1. Stabilize reproducible releases and define party, currency and financial revision contracts.
  2. Complete intake evidence, effective revisions, pricing correctness and facility-backed capacity.
  3. Enforce eligibility and integrate existing quote/bind workflows with obligations and billing.
  4. Prove the insurer pilot across two unlike configured risk classes.
  5. Complete marketplace actions, funding/collateral reconciliation, claim economics and payouts before a capital release.

Cross-cutting dependencies

AIN-34 (party subledger), AIN-35 (money/currency) and AIN-18 (revision lineage) support numerous domain workflows. AIN-47 supplies schema design and enforcement tasks. AIN-45 owns financial conservation/failure-injection coverage, while AIN-55 owns schema/constraint/migration checks. AIN-12 records staged acceptance; it does not substitute for the domain evidence.

Ownership and estimates

No owners, sprint commitments, numerical service objectives or release dates were assigned during these reviews. Those decisions should follow task sizing, provider selection, approved launch scope and available delivery capacity. Consult live Jira for status; this roadmap summarizes scope rather than a continuously synchronized status board.
