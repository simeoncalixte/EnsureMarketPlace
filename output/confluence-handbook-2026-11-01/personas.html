Insurer pilot journey

  1. Authenticate, establish permitted organization context and complete required onboarding/evidence checks.
  2. Configure a risk class, schema, appetite and model/rating parameters.
  3. Receive documents; process them into structured evidence and generated sections.
  4. Review/correct the workbench; preserve the selected source revision and feedback history.
  5. Run underwriting/modeling, inspect uncertainty, resolve capacity and capture an approved quote snapshot.
  6. Deliver terms, record acceptance/signatures and use the existing binding workflow where permitted.
  7. Hand the bound financial and contractual evidence to servicing and billing; close the remaining handoff gaps through Jira.

Capital release journey

Publish an eligible opportunity → inspect immutable documents and risk assumptions → propose/approve participation → create a commitment → call and confirm funds → allocate/collateralize → service premiums and claims → approve and reconcile distributions. Several transitions currently have only partial implementation or data models; AIN-8 through AIN-10 own completion.

Exception journeys

  • Unreadable/conflicting documents remain reviewable rather than silently becoming zero/known facts.
  • Expired eligibility, changed terms, unavailable capacity and failed providers must block or refer the relevant action.
  • Corrections and reversals append history; actors should never “fix” settled money by deleting records.
