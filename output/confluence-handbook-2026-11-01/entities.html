Relationship decisions to formalize

  • Canonical party/account IDs and role assignments, including how individuals, organizations, SPVs and beneficiaries relate.
  • Same-tenant compound references and service-boundary reference validation.
  • A versioned money/currency contract shared across pricing, capital, servicing and fees.
  • Immutable source/version links plus transaction-safe current pointers and operation uniqueness.
  • Explicit data-retention, evidence classification and model ownership.

As-is versus target

This is a logical catalog derived from code, not a complete physical ERD. AIN-48 must produce as-is/to-be diagrams and a field dictionary; AIN-49 must inventory existing constraints before adding missing ones. Preserve working commercial constraints and additive compatibility.
