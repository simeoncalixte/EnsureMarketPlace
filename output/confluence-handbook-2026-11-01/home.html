Reviewed November 1, 2026. Status: current implementation and planned work are distinguished below. Documentation is not certification of production readiness.

Ensured is an insurance and reinsurance platform whose first goal is to help insurers, MGAs, brokers and cedents turn fragmented submissions into reviewable underwriting decisions. Its longer-term goal is to connect approved risks with eligible capital and support the resulting contracts, servicing and financial obligations.

Project state at this review

The repository contains working foundations for accounts/gateway, KYC review, document processing, generated submission forms, human feedback, modeling/pricing, commercial records, quotes/signatures/binding and network collaboration. This does not establish a complete end-to-end production product. Party financial reconciliation, funding/settlement, model input correctness, release reproducibility and several marketplace actions remain tracked work.

Evidence and terminology

  • Implemented means a relevant source path or documented service exists; it does not imply deployed, fully tested or legally approved.
  • Verified means a specific check was actually run and its scope is named. Planned means acceptance criteria are tracked in Jira.
  • Capacity is permission/availability to assume risk. A commitment is a promise. Funding is received capital. Allocation/deployment is use of capital. Settlement is a confirmed financial transfer.
  • Seed fixtures, illustrative metrics, snapshots, contract approvals and provider acceptance must not be described as proof of paid cash or production readiness.
