Existing capacity accounting

Commercial capacity entries record reserve, allocate, release, refer and close_referral. Exact cent arithmetic calculates outstanding reservations and allocations. Allocation against a reservation moves usage rather than adding a second exposure; releases reference an earlier entry. Aggregate, risk and occurrence limits constrain usage. This is risk-capacity accounting, not bank settlement.

Existing servicing accounting

Servicing stores report obligations, premium due/paid, claims, claim reductions, recoveries and renewals. Summaries group by currency and contract: premium outstanding = due − paid; recognized claims = claims − reductions; recoverable = recognized claims − recoveries. These calculations do not determine treaty entitlement from a gross loss and do not identify both payer/payee accounts.

Target premium and capital flows

  1. Capture gross premium, commission/tax/fee basis and currency from approved terms.
  2. Create linked party receivables/payables with exact contract/revision and due date.
  3. Record provider-confirmed receipts separately from promises and allocate them to obligations.
  4. Distribute net amounts under the approved rules, preserving rounding residuals and evidence.
  5. Reconcile statements and append reversals/corrections when needed; never rewrite historical payments.

Illustrative conservation example — not a product tariff

If an approved USD 1,000 premium has a USD 100 intermediary commission and USD 20 platform fee, the remaining USD 880 is attributable to the contractual recipient before any separately defined tax or other split. All three sides must total USD 1,000. This example explains conservation only; actual terms, payer direction, tax treatment and recognition policy must be configured and approved.

Confirmed display issues and missing capabilities

Investor dashboards currently count pending commitments as funded/invested and include unowned opportunities in a weighted return labeled IRR. Capital models lack consistent currency fields. Target party ledgers, commission semantics, settlement reconciliation and immutable payment reversals are tracked separately.
