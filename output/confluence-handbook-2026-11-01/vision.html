Mission

Make an underwriting team productive before the marketplace has significant capital liquidity. The primary value is turning messy inbound risk information into consistent, reviewable submissions with explicit evidence, appetite decisions, loss estimates and pricing. Capital matching is an expansion of this foundation, not a prerequisite for useful insurer software.

Strategic goals

  1. Unify document/email/portal intake and reduce repeated manual transcription without discarding source evidence.
  2. Support any configured risk class through taxonomy, organization-editable schema and selected modeling/rating methods.
  3. Make every financial or risk number explainable through method, inputs, assumptions and revision history.
  4. Provide human review and explicit approvals for underwriting, signatures, binding and financial actions.
  5. Connect eligible counterparties to governed facilities, commitments and ultimately reconciled settlement and servicing.

Business model direction

The near-term roadmap identifies organization/seat subscriptions and usage-based services as the insurer-side revenue opportunity. Marketplace placement fees, asset-management fees, trading and data products are later possibilities, not all implemented revenue streams. Technical premium is the cost of transferred risk; it must not be confused with platform revenue.

Architectural principles

  • Risk class is configuration/data, not a special-case engine branch for each line of business.
  • Extend working models and use additive, backward-compatible migrations.
  • Pin immutable reviewed versions so a schema or data correction cannot silently change past decisions.
  • Use AI assistance with clear provenance and explicit fallback behavior; do not imply unsupported confidence or automatic approval.
  • Keep tenant authorization, identity ownership and evidence handling at explicit service boundaries.
