Later product expansionAdditional lines, vendors, analytics and secondary-market capabilitiesSeparate product decisions and validation; model/table existence is insufficient.

What completion means

  • A fresh checkout builds compatible services and packages with the required checks passing.
  • Each demonstrated outcome uses actual persisted workflow evidence rather than seed/demo shortcuts.
  • Financial figures distinguish estimate, commitment, receivable/payable, receipt, deployment and settlement.
  • Reviewers can reproduce a decision from its immutable inputs, schema, model and approval history.
  • Tenant separation, concurrency, provider failure, restore and rollback are tested with documented results.

Measurement plan

Measure intake success by format, extraction correction rate, time from intake to reviewed quote, failed/retried jobs, model input coverage, quote-to-bind completion, reconciliation exceptions, latency and processing cost. Establish baselines and agree thresholds before claiming a target is met; no numerical launch SLA has been approved by this review.

Scope boundaries

EnsuredLogistics is a separate freight collaboration product in the workspace. It is not the insurance transaction lifecycle and should have its own product/release documentation. Licenses, regulatory acceptance, external vendor contracts and custody arrangements require accountable business decisions. This handbook does not assert they have been obtained.
