Required provenance

A reproducible financial result needs the selected risk class/method, canonical/effective revision, exposure basis and currency, prior source/citation, model parameters, model version, simulated seed when relevant, confidence/flags and reviewer decision. A bare decimal or chart is insufficient.

Confirmed correctness defects

  • deriveExposure currently selects TIV before revenue/payroll even when another basis is declared; the isolated revenue fixture produced a tenfold exposure error.
  • Unavailable technicalPremium becomes numeric zero in persistence; some consumers mask flags but the value remains ambiguous. Legitimate approved zero-price terms must remain distinguishable.
  • ModelRun/PricingVersion/current pointer/metrics are written without one encompassing transaction, creating a partial-write/concurrency risk; database fault-injection remains to be done.

Validation and governance

Maintain known-answer fixtures, parameter bounds, currency/basis checks and independent actuarial review for launch methods. Import only qualified experience with valuation periods and gross/net meaning. Model approval, quote approval, contractual binding and payment execution are different decisions and should retain separate evidence.
